Tajweed feedback

It listens to how you recite — not only what

An acoustic model compares every word you say with its expected Ḥafṣ pronunciation, sound by sound — on your phone, without internet.

  • Maddelongations held their full length
  • Qalqalathe echo of ق ط ب ج د
  • Ghunnahikhfāʾ, iqlāb and idghām
  • Heavy & lightط ت · ص س · ض د · ظ ذ · ق ك
  • Throat lettersء ع ح ه خ غ
  • Shadda & vowelsdoubled letters, ḥarakāt, case endings

The model is young and still learning: it may miss a mistake or flag a correct word. Every version gets better — your feedback helps.
